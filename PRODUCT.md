# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective and current Loyola Computer Science students, faculty, researchers,
and collaborators who want to understand the AI4FM group, find its research and
software, or learn how to get involved.

## Product Purpose

AI4FM is the public research-group site for the AI for Formal Methods group at
Loyola University Chicago. It makes the group's publications, updates, people,
software, datasets, and opportunities easy to discover and reuse.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, open, grounded. The site should feel academically credible without
being formal for its own sake, and technically serious without becoming opaque.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS marketing patterns, decorative gradients, and personal or
uncontextualized photography in the homepage hero carousel. Research artifacts,
posters, and diagrams should carry the visual story when imagery is needed.

## Design Principles

- Show the work: let publications, artifacts, and reproducible tools lead.
- Practice openness: make research outputs and ways to participate easy to find.
- Keep the academic context visible without burying the reader in institutional language.
- Use Loyola visual cues with restraint and enough contrast for comfortable reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, responsive layouts, readable
line lengths, descriptive text for meaningful images, and reduced-motion
preferences. Decorative carousel imagery must remain hidden from assistive
technology.
